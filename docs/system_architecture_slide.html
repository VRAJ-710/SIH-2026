OCEAN-3D: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & END-TO-END EXECUTION FLOWS

High-Performance Ocean Digital Twin Integrating CF-1.8 NetCDF Ocean Models & Real In-Situ Observations

Scenario: Super Cyclone Amphan
Domain: 8–23°N, 82–92°E (181×121×50)
Stack: TDS:8080 + FastAPI:8000 + React/Cesium:5173
1. Ingestion & Data Standardization CF-1.8 & Parquet
GLORYS12 Numerical Model: 113.9 MB NetCDF-4, 50 non-uniform depth levels, ~8km (1/12°) resolution across 13 days for Temp, Salinity & Currents.
In-Situ Multi-Platform Feeds: 30 unique instruments (17,917 rows) from Argo floats (BGC Chl-a), INCOIS OMNI buoys, Seaglider, ADCP moorings & CTD casts.
Unified Columnar Storage: 8-column normalized Parquet schema (points_*.parquet) for sub-millisecond in-memory columnar filtering.
2. High-Performance Serving Tier Dockerized Microservices
Unidata THREDDS Server (5.8): Runs in sih_tds:8080. Dynamic ncWMS 1.3.0 GetMap tile slicing in CRS:84 directly from 4D NetCDF.
FastAPI Analytical Backend: Runs in sih_backend:8000. Thread-safe in-memory stores for profiles and subgrid extraction (15–35ms latency).
Collocation & Interpolation: Real-time bilinear horizontal and 1D linear depth interpolation for instant model validation against sensor depths.
3. Interactive Presentation Tier WebGL & Hardware Accelerated
CesiumJS 3D Globe: WMS dynamic raster overlay with bathymetry shading + 6-second zero-hang offline fallback to local NaturalEarthII tiles.
Three.js Subsurface Engine: Real-time GPU volume raymarching with 3D textures + Marching Cubes isosurface and bloom shading at 60 FPS.
Plotly Depth Profiler: Collocated observed vs. modeled curves, live MAE error badges, and 5-beat Super Cyclone Amphan guided temporal tour.
